A table set for the people you love

Savory House began as a tiny kitchen with one oven and a simple idea: cook honest food from whatever the season offers, and take the time to do it properly.

Today our chefs still work with local farms and fishermen, finish most dishes over open flame, and change the menu as the weather turns. Come for a quiet dinner, a birthday, or a long Sunday lunch.
